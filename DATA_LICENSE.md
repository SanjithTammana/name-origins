[Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/) content, extracted by [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/) (wiktextract),
and are licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Attribution: Wiktionary contributors. Each name's entry links to its Wiktionary page, where the full edit
history and authorship are available.

Changes: entries were filtered to given names; etymologies were reduced to an origin chain, a meaning and a
short summary; the origin model, word vectors and similarity lists were computed from that text.
